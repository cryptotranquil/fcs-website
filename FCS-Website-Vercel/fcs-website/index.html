About FCS

For more than a decade we have helped Malawi’s family-owned and growing businesses turn scattered records into *bank-ready* accounts, building the systems and *discipline* that let good enterprises *grow.*

Many SMEs don’t have the structures to track their day-to-day activities, which makes it hard to build relationships with banks, the revenue authority and customers. FCS closes that gap.

We believe that when SMEs have strong governance, Malawi’s whole economy benefits. These businesses have great skill and creativity that can drive national growth.

Corporate Philosophy
Every enterprise can reach its goals if it has the right systems and the right attitude for organic growth.
i.

Systems

Governance, bookkeeping and reporting structures that keep track of every day’s business and build a record you can be proud of.

ii.

Attitude

Every corporate failure comes from choices made by its owners. We give businesses a platform to survive and grow through better choices.

iii.

Skills

Systems are only as good as the people who run them. We build management and accounting competence through hands-on training.

We also offer accountancy career development, marketing and customer and employee satisfaction surveys, so our clients can align their objectives with the people they serve.
